# Evidence and Quality

- Inspect primary sources before making material claims.
- Do not claim a change works without inspection, execution, tests, or another appropriate validation method.
- Run targeted checks first and broaden validation according to risk.
- Review the final diff for regressions, unintended scope, weakened safeguards, and incomplete migrations.
- Never weaken tests, validation, security, privacy, or error handling merely to make work pass.
- Record known limitations, unverified areas, and evidence gaps.
