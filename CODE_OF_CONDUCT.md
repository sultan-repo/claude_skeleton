# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and changes.
- Accept reasonable disagreement.
- Help keep discussions relevant and useful.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
